    Questions

    Questions

    What does VoiceRouter do?

    It turns what you say into organized work. Each spoken note is transcribed, matched to the right project, table or doc, and filed once you confirm.

    How do I see where a note went?

    Every match shows its destination and a confidence score. You can accept it or move it, and everything stays searchable.

    Does my voice go to the cloud?

    You choose: transcription can run on your device or in the cloud.

    Can one note be filed in several places?

    Yes. A note has one main home and can link to other projects or docs.

    Is VoiceRouter a dictation app?

    Not quite. Dictation gives you text; VoiceRouter also decides where that text should live.
